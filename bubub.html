My Letter to You 💕

To my dearest, Alissa,

happy 1st monthsary, bub! You probably don't expect me to write you a letter, bub, because it's kind of a new thing to me, but here I am writing you my first-ever letter for you that is somewhat decent (?). Isn't it ironic that when we were first still together, you used to write me letters, and at that time, I still didn't have any idea how to write anything? But it is also because of you that I started to learn how to write letters and poems.

As I look back to the past, bub, I sometimes wonder if I actually miss you or just the feeling since at times I look for you in all of the places that we once were, and then there are times I don't care if you were there at all. I spent years longing for you, but I thought to myself, "Do I actually miss you, or is it just the feeling and not you?" But I'm certain that what I long for is you.

I actually thought that we would never be with each other again and that we would slowly drift apart as if we were never even a thing, that you would eventually find someone and move on with your life and I wouldn't even know about it. I started to think of not marrying anyone and thought maybe I was never meant to stay not in your heart, not in your way.

But then we met each other again and the first time I saw you again bub I know that my heart still beats for you and all of the feelings keep rushing back and after the day that I saw you all I could think of was you just like what virginia woolf said "I can't tell you how piercingly and endlessly I think about you" that's why I wanted to stay in gensan for a while because I believe that there's still something between us and I cling to that hope.

I'm very happy because despite all of the paths I could've crossed it somehow leads me back to you and I question "why out of all the places must I be here?" I think that the purpose why I was in gensan was for Heavenly Father to bring us back together. It's truly a privilege to be loved by you, bub, who's not only so beautiful but also such a beautiful, kind soul. I saw you grow into a better person, bub, and how you handle different things; even if it doesn't feel right, you stay calm and composed. I hope that you will continue to grow to be the best version of yourself, and I pray for us to grow as a couple, I can already see my life with you, bub (so excited to marry you bub hehe). I just want your presence, bub. I want to hear your voice, laugh, smell your scent, and see you smile—I just want to grace your entire existence. It's just sad that we would part ways for a while; it's not even a while because it's years 😭. I will miserably miss you, bub; my heart aches just thinking about it, but I will bottle it up because I need to do the Lord's work. I long for the time that I could hold your hand, caress your hair, and feel the warmth of you again. I hope to celebrate our monthsaries and anniversaries together very soon, I love you forever bub.

Yours always,
Ian
